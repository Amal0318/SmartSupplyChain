# 👥 Stakeholders Directory

## Internal Stakeholders
- **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**: High-level executive performance visibility and strategic KPI oversight.
- **Operations Manager**: Cross-departmental operational workflow oversight and bottleneck resolution.
- **Supply Chain Manager**: End-to-end supply chain efficiency, inventory levels, and logistics optimization.
- **Procurement Manager & Executives**: Sourcing, supplier negotiation, PR/PO approvals, and vendor management.
- **Warehouse Manager & Staff**: Receiving, storage, bin management, inventory transfers, and material issue.
- **Inventory Controller**: Stock audit monitoring, reorder point enforcement, and inventory accuracy.
- **Production Manager & Supervisors**: Work order execution, BOM management, and production tracking.
- **Quality Inspector**: Materials receiving quality verification, defect recording, and return authorizations.
- **Logistics Manager**: Freight dispatch, carrier allocation, and delivery status tracking.
- **Finance Manager**: 3-Way invoice matching, vendor payments, and procurement budget monitoring.
- **System Administrator**: RBAC permission management, master data administration, and system maintenance.

## External Stakeholders
- **Suppliers / Vendors**: PO confirmation, shipping updates, quotation submission, invoice generation.
- **Carriers / Freight Providers**: Shipment pickup, location tracking feeds, delivery status updates.
- **Customers**: Order delivery tracking and receiving confirmation.
